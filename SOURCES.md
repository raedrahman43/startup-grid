## Interpretation
Amounts are per announced transaction, not lifetime capital raised. The first clue means an announced transaction of at least $100M, including announced agreements and primary/secondary mixes. Stripe's announcement describes signed agreements and employee liquidity/tax purposes, not a confirmed closing. Mercury's amount includes secondary sales, not solely new company capital. Both caveats appear in the game and directory.

Investor clues refer only to participation in the selected announcement. Andreessen Horowitz is normalized to a16z. Investor lists are partial, but every accepted investor match is supported by the linked announcement. No unlisted investor is asserted to have never invested in a company. Examples of overlapping valid answers: ElevenLabs matches all three AI columns; Cursor matches two coding columns; Mercury matches all three fintech columns. The game validates predicates rather than one predetermined answer.

Real-company controversies and talent clues remain planned content; they require separate dated evidence and review. None are invented for this dataset. All sponsor placements remain generic demo inventory; company inclusion does not imply endorsement or sponsorship.
